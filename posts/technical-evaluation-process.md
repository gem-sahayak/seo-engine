Government e-Marketplace (GeM) portal aur public procurement tenders me bid submission ke baad sabse pehla aur sabse critical phase hota hai **Technical Evaluation Process**.

Kafi sellers ko lagta hai ki pricing kam rakhne se contract mil jayega, lekin reality yeh hai ki jab tak aapki bid Technical Scrutiny pass nahi karti, aapki Financial Price Bid open bhi nahi ki jaati.

Is detailed guide me hum **Technical Evaluation Stages**, Committee Scrutiny Rules, Document Verification Checklist, aur 48-Hour Representation Appeal System ko detail me samjhenge.

## 1. Technical Evaluation Process Step-by-Step

GeM portal par bid end date finish hone ke baad Buyer Tender Evaluation Committee ke dashboard par technical bids unlock hoti hain:

```
Step 1: Tender Opening & Bid Unencrypted Decryption
Step 2: Document & Qualification Verification (Past Experience, Turnover, GST, PAN)
Step 3: Technical Parameter & BOQ Specification Matching
Step 4: Exemption Proof Validation (Udyam NIC Code & DPIIT Startup Proof)
Step 5: Publishing Technical Qualification / Disqualification Summary
Step 6: 48-Hour Seller Representation Window
```

---

## 2. Key Scrutiny Checkpoints in Technical Evaluation

Buyer Scrutiny Committee aapke uploaded documents me niche diye gaye 5 major checkpoints verify karti hai:

### A. Past Experience & Performance Certificates
Tender terms ke mutabiq past 3 financial years me similar work orders execute karne ka proof (jaise *Client Completion Certificates* ya *GeM CRAC Reports*).

### B. CA Financial Turnover Certificates (With UDIN)
Chartered Accountant dwara certified annual turnover reports. Har CA certificate par valid 18-digit **UDIN (Unique Document Identification Number)** hona compulsory hai.

### C. Udyam & EMD Exemption Proof Validation
Agar seller ne EMD ya Annual Turnover Waiver claim kiya hai, toh system check karta hai ki:
- Seller **Micro or Small Enterprise (MSE)** status hold karta hai ya nahi.
- Udyam Certificate par tender item ka exact **5-Digit NIC Code** listed hai ya nahi.

### D. OEM Authorization Form (MAF)
Reseller sellers ke case me Manufacturer Authorization Certificate format tender terms ke according customized hona chahiye.

## 3. Technical Disqualification & The 48-Hour Representation Window

Technical Evaluation complete hone ke baad Buyer System par **Qualified** aur **Disqualified** bidders ki list publish karta hai.

### Representation Appeal Rules:
- **Time Limit:** Disqualification notice publish hone ke exact **48 Hours** ke andar seller online appeal submit kar sakta hai.
- **How to Submit:** GeM Seller Portal $\rightarrow$ My Bids $\rightarrow$ View Technical Evaluation Status $\rightarrow$ Click **Submit Representation**.
- **Process:** Seller official clarification write-up aur supporting proof documents attach karke Buyer Committee ko rejection decision review karne ke liye contest karta hai.

Rejection reasons aur representation letter formats ke liye humara comprehensive guide [Top Reasons for Bid Rejection on GeM](/knowledge/compliance-policy/reasons-for-bid-rejection) aur [Representation Writing Guide](/knowledge/gem-bidding/representation-writing) padhein.

## Technical Scrutiny Verification Matrix

| Document / Parameter | Verification Criteria | Common Disqualification Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Udyam Certificate** | Active MSE Status + Matching NIC Code | NIC Code not matching tender category |
| **CA Turnover Report** | Average Turnover Threshold + UDIN | UDIN missing on CA Certificate |
| **EMD Instrument** | BG / DD Validity + Required Amount | BG validity shorter than required period |
| **BOQ Compliance** | Signed Technical Spec Compliance Sheet | Unsigned or deviation in golden parameters |
