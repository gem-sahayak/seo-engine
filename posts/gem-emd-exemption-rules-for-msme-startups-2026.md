# GeM EMD Exemption Rules for MSMEs & Startups (2026): GFR Rule 170 Waiver Guide

Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) ya Bid Security har government tender mein bid participation ke liye submit karni hoti hai. Lekin General Financial Rules (GFR 2017) Rule 170 ke under **Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs)** aur **DPIIT Recognized Startups** ko 100% EMD waiver ka privilege diya gaya hai.

Is comprehensive guide mein hum samjhenge **GeM EMD Exemption claim karne ke rules**, **Udyam NIC Code matching requirements**, aur **disqualification se bachne ke tarike**.

---

## 📜 GFR 2017 Rule 170 & Policy Circular Overview

Central Government Public Procurement Policy 2012 (amended 2026) ke mutabiq:

- **Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs)** jo Ministry of MSME / Udyam portal par registered hain, unhe sabhi Central Ministries, Departments, aur CPSEs ke GeM bids mein EMD submit karne se Exemption prapt hai.
- **DPIIT Recognized Startups** ko bhi Turnover aur Prior Experience exemptions ke saath 100% EMD waiver milta hai.

---

## 📋 Summary Eligibility Matrix for EMD Exemption

| Entity Type | Eligible for EMD Exemption? | Required Document | Mandatory Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Micro Enterprise** | ✅ YES (100% Exempted) | Valid Udyam Certificate | NIC 5-Digit code must match Bid Category |
| **Small Enterprise** | ✅ YES (100% Exempted) | Valid Udyam Certificate | NIC 5-Digit code must match Bid Category |
| **Medium Enterprise** | ❌ NO (Standard EMD Applies) | Udyam Certificate | Exempted only if tender specifically allows |
| **DPIIT Startup** | ✅ YES (100% Exempted) | DPIIT Recognition Certificate | Valid DIPP Number & Active Status |
| **Trader / Reseller** | ⚠️ CONDITIONAL | Udyam Certificate | OEM Exemption / Reseller policy allowed by buyer |

---

## ⚠️ The Critical NIC Code Matching Rule (Why Bids Get Disqualified)

Sabse zyada MSME bids is wajah se reject hoti hain ki unka Udyam Certificate toh valid hota hai, lekin **Udyam certificate par added 5-digit NIC Code tender ke product/service category se match nahi karta**.

**Example:**
- Tender Category: *Security Guard Supply Services (NIC 78102)*
- Seller Udyam NIC Code: *Manufacture of Furniture (NIC 31001)*
- **Result**: Buyer bid evaluation screen par seller ka EMD Exemption Claim **REJECT** ho jayega aur bid technical evaluation mein disqualify ho jayegi.

> 💡 **Solution**: Bid participate karne se pehle official Udyam portal par jaakar relevant 5-Digit NIC Code zaroor add karein.

```text
DECLARATION FOR EMD EXEMPTION (UNDER GFR 2017 RULE 170)

To,
The Procurement Officer / Buyer,
[Department Name / Ministry],

Re: Bid Number [GEM/2026/B/XXXXXXX] Dated [DD/MM/YYYY]

Dear Sir/Madam,

We, M/s [Your Firm Name], hereby declare that we are registered as a Micro/Small Enterprise under the Ministry of MSME with Udyam Registration Number [UDYAM-XX-00-0000000].

As per Public Procurement Policy for MSEs Order 2012 & GFR 2017 Rule 170, we are claiming 100% Exemption from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD). Our Udyam Certificate carrying relevant NIC Code [XXXXX] is attached herewith.

Thanking You,
For M/s [Your Firm Name]
[Authorized Signatory & Seal]
```

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Kya Traders ko GeM EMD exemption milta hai?
Public Procurement Policy ke mutabiq EMD Exemption primarily Manufacturers aur Service Providers ke liye hai. Single-category Traders ke case mein buyer tender clause ke according decide hota hai.

### Q2: EMD Exemption lene par Bid Security Declaration upload karna mandatory hai?
Haan, GeM portal par EMD Exempted sellers ko Annexure-based Bid Security Declaration (jisme Likha hota hai ki bid withdraw karne par 2 saal ke liye debar kar diya jayega) accept karna hota hai.
