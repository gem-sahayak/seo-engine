## 1. Daily Tender Monitoring Kya Hai Aur Yeh Kyun Zaroori Hai?

Daily Tender Monitoring ka matlab hai GeM portal par apne business vertical, product category, aur target locations se jude naye publicado tenders ko rozana track karna.

### MSMEs ke liye Daily Monitoring ke 4 Main Advantages:

1. **Short-Window Tenders Miss Nahi Hote:** GeM par 10 Lakh INR se kam ke kai tenders 7 se 10 dino ke short timeframe ke liye live aate hain. Agar aap hafte me ek baar search karenge, toh deadline nikal chuki hogi.
2. **Corrigendum (Amendment Notices) Track Rehte Hain:** Buyer departments pre-bid queries ke baad tender specifications, delivery timelines, ya EMD clauses me badlaav karte hain. Daily monitoring se aapko in updates ka turant pata chalta hai.
3. **Representation Window Skip Nahi Hoti:** Agar aapka technical bid reject ho jata hai, toh GeM rules ke mutabiq aapko appeal (Representation) bhejne ke liye sirf **48 hours** ka time milta hai. Daily dashboard check karne se aap time rehte appeal file kar sakte hain.
4. **Competitor Strategy Analysis:** Aapko pata chalta hai ki aapki category me konse buyers frequent purchases kar rahe hain aur konse competitors bids jeet rahe hain.

## 2. GeM Tender Monitoring Ke 4 Main Search Vectors

GeM Portal par har din hazaron bids hoti hain. Sahi tenders dhundne ke liye aapko in 4 parameters par monitoring setup karni chahiye:

### A. Keyword-Based Monitoring (Product & Service Specs)
Aapke product ke exact HSN codes, technical parameters, aur common Industry names (jaise *Modular Office Chairs*, *CCTV Surveillance Camera*, *Outsourcing Manpower*) ke exact keywords se searches save karein. Simple general words ke bajaye specific technical terms use karein.

### B. Ministry & Department Tracking
Aapke target public buyers (jaise *Ministry of Defence*, *CPWD*, *Indian Railways*, *State Health Society*) ke specific departments ko follow karein. Unke upcoming annual procurement plans aur recurring service tenders hamesha priority par rakhein.

### C. Category & Sub-Category Matching
GeM par har item specific Golden Parameters aur Official Category Path ke under listed hota hai. Apni catalog listing category ke according search queries filter karein taaki irrelevant bids filter out ho jayein.

### D. Consignee Location (State & City Level)
GeM tender search me Consignee delivery location filter set karein. Isse aapko apne state ya neighboring districts ke tenders pehle dikhenge, jahan logistically deliver karna easy hota hai.

GeM search filters ko detail me samajhne ke liye humari [GeM Tender Search Guide](/knowledge/gem-bidding/gem-tender-search-guide) zaroor padhein.

## 3. Recommended 20-Minute Morning Routine For Bid Managers

Ek disciplined daily workflow se aapka time bachega aur bidding efficiency 3x ho jayegi:

```
Step 1: Check WhatsApp & Email Tender Alerts (08:30 AM - 08:35 AM)
Step 2: Log in to GeM Portal & Scan Saved Searches (08:35 AM - 08:45 AM)
Step 3: Review Corrigendums & Disqualification Status (08:45 AM - 08:50 AM)
Step 4: Update Internal Tender Pipeline Tracker (08:50 AM - 08:50 AM)
```

### Detailed Step-by-Step Routine:

* **Step 1 — Morning Alert Review:** SahayakAI WhatsApp Notification panel ya saved search alerts check karein taaki raat bhar me aayi nai bids ka digest mil jaye.
* **Step 2 — Saved Search Execution:** GeM Portal par log in karke apne Saved Search Configurations run karein. Filter set karke "Published Date: Last 24 Hours" select karein.
* **Step 3 — Active Bids Corrigendum Check:** Jin bids me aap participate karne wale hain, unke Tender ID par click karke Corrigendum tab check karein. Agar Buyer ne specs change kiye hain, toh apni BOQ compliance sheet ko turant update karein.
* **Step 4 — Post-Submission Technical Scrutiny:** Jo bids aap submit kar chuke hain, unka Technical Evaluation status dekhein. Agar buyer ne bid **"Evaluated"** status me daal di hai, toh check karein ki aapka status Qualified hai ya Rejection reasons show ho rahe hain.

## 4. Post-Submission Monitoring: 48-Hour Representation Window

Bidding process me sabse bada loss tab hota hai jab MSME seller valid eligibility hone ke bawajood technical evaluation me galat disqualfiy kar diya jata hai, aur seller late check karne ki wajah se appeal nahi kar pata.

GeM system me Buyer dwara Technical Disqualification notification aane ke baad seller ko system-generated **48 Hours** ka window milta hai online Representation file karne ke liye. 

Daily monitoring ka sabse bada benefit yahi hai ki aap instantly representation draft karke buyer panel par contest kar sakte hain. Detailed representation formats aur rejection appeals ke liye humara article [GeM Disqualification Representation Writing Guide](/knowledge/gem-bidding/representation-writing) padhein.

## 5. Common Tender Monitoring Mistakes MSMEs Make

1. **Hafte Me Ek Baar Search Karna:** Isse short-duration bids aur critical pre-bid dates chhoot jaati hain.
2. **Sirf Exact Match Title Dekhna:** Pehle Buyer Custom Bid (BOQ) bana dete hain jahan Title generic hota hai. Description aur Attached PDF padhna zaroori hai.
3. **Corrigendum Ignore Karna:** Bid submission se 2 ghante pehle final document check na karna. Aksar EMD amount ya delivery period tab tak revise ho chuka hota hai.
4. **Internal Excel Tracker Na Banana:** Bids ki End Date, EMD submission status aur Security Deposit dates ka record manual tarike se track na karna.
