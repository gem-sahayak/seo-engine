# How to Claim EMD Waiver in CPPP Tenders for MSME Sellers

Under the **Public Procurement Policy for Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) Order**, eligible Micro and Small Enterprises registered under **Udyam** or NSIC are entitled to **100% exemption from paying Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)** and Tender Processing Fees on central government tenders published on the **Central Public Procurement Portal (CPPP - eprocure.gov.in)**.

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on claiming EMD waivers legally and resolving tender rejection risks.

---

## 1. Eligibility Criteria for EMD Exemption on CPPP

To qualify for EMD and tender fee waivers under Public Procurement Rules:

| Criteria | Eligible Category | Non-Eligible Category |
|---|---|---|
| **Enterprise Size** | Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs) | Medium Enterprises / Large Corporates |
| **Registration Type** | Udyam Registration / NSIC Certificate | Unregistered Traders / Informal Entities |
| **Product / Service Match** | Tender items match Udyam NIC Activity Code | Tender items outside registered NIC code scope |
| **Document Required** | Valid Udyam Certificate & EMD Exemption Declaration | Missing or expired Udyam document |

---

## 2. Step-by-Step EMD Exemption Claim Process on CPPP

```text
[Select Tender on eprocure.gov.in] ➔ [Click 'Seek EMD Exemption'] ➔ [Select 'Yes'] ➔ [Upload Udyam Certificate] ➔ [Attach Bid Securing Declaration] ➔ [Freeze Bid]
```

### Step 1: Verify Tender Notice Terms
1. Open the Notice Inviting Tender (NIT) document on `eprocure.gov.in`.
2. Check Section **"EMD Exemption Allowed: YES / NO"**.
3. Confirm that MSE exemptions are explicitly authorized in the tender parameters.

### Step 2: Initiate Bid Submission
1. Log into `eprocure.gov.in/eprocure/app` with your vendor credentials.
2. Search for the tender ID and click **"Enroll in Tender"** ➔ **"Proceed to Bid Submission"**.

### Step 3: Select EMD Exemption Option
1. In the fee payment screen, select **"Seeking EMD Exemption: YES"**.
2. Select your exemption authority (e.g., **UDYAM / NSIC / KVIC**).

### Step 4: Upload Supporting Documents
1. Upload a clear PDF copy of your **Udyam Registration Certificate**.
2. Upload a signed **Bid Securing Declaration** on corporate letterhead (stating that if your firm withdraws the bid during validity, you agree to temporary suspension from government tenders).

### Step 5: Freeze and Submit Bid
1. Upload technical and financial envelopes (BOQ).
2. Click **"Freeze Bid Submission"** and sign the submission using your Class 3 DSC token.

## 3. Common Reasons EMD Exemption Claims Are Rejected

### Reason A: Mismatch in NIC Activity Code
If your Udyam certificate lists NIC codes for "IT Services" but you bid for "Furniture Supply", the procurement officer may disqualify the exemption claim.
- **Solution:** Ensure your Udyam profile includes all relevant NIC codes prior to tender submission.

### Reason B: Submission by Medium Enterprises
Medium enterprises under MSMED Act are **NOT eligible** for EMD waivers under the MSE Policy Order.
- **Solution:** Verify your classification status on the Udyam portal.

### Reason C: Failure to Attach Bid Securing Declaration
Many central departments mandate a signed Bid Securing Declaration format in lieu of EMD.
- **Solution:** Always download the exact Annexure format specified in the tender document.
