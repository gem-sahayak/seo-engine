Government tenders me bidding ka sabse crucial phase hota hai **Financial Bid Evaluation**. Jab bidders Technical Evaluation stage cross kar lete hain, tab Buyer Evaluation Committee unke financial rates (commercial bids) open karti hai.

GeM (Government e-Marketplace) portal par pricing scrutiny fully automated aur transparent hoti hai. Is complete guide me hum **Financial Bid Evaluation Rules**, landed price calculation, GST components, aur L1 generation process ko detail me samjhenge.

## 1. Financial Bid Opening Stage Kab Hota Hai?

GeM portal par 2-cover bidding system follow hota hai:
- **Cover 1:** Technical Bid (Eligibility, Certificates, Specifications).
- **Cover 2:** Financial / Commercial Price Bid.

Financial Bid Tab tab tak password-protected aur encrypted rehti hai jab tak Buyer Committee sabhi submitted bids ka Technical Evaluation complete karke portal par status freeze nahi kar deti.

```
Step 1: Technical Scrutiny & Committee Approval
Step 2: Publishing Technical Evaluation Summary
Step 3: 48-Hour Seller Representation Window
Step 4: System-Unlocking of Commercial Bids (Financial Opening)
```

## 2. Pricing Scrutiny Rules & Landed Cost Calculation

GeM Portal price comparison pure item rate par nahi, balki **Total Landed Cost to Consignee** par karta hai.

### Landed Price Components:
1. **Basic Unit Price:** Product ya Service ka base rate.
2. **Freight & Inland Logistics:** Delivery location tak shipping/transportation cost.
3. **Applicable GST Slabs:** Item category ke according 5%, 12%, 18%, ya 28% GST rate.
4. **On-Site Installation & Warranty:** Equipment tenders me installation & extended warranty charges.

$$\text{Evaluated Landed Price } (P_L) = \text{Quantity} \times (\text{Unit Base Price} + \text{Freight Charges}) \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{GST \%}}{100}\right)$$

## 3. Abnormally Low Financial Bids (ALFB Rules)

Public procurement guidelines (GFR Rule 173) ke under, agar koi bidder market cost se bohot zyada sasti price (Abnormally Low Bid) quote karta hai, toh buyer bid instantly accept nahi karta.

### Buyer Action in Abnormally Low Bids:
- Buyer Committee bidder se official **Financial Justification Letter** mangti hai.
- Bidder ko proof dena padta hai ki woh execution quality compromise kiye bina is rate par deliver kaise karega.
- Satisfactory reply na milne par committee bid reject kar sakti hai.
