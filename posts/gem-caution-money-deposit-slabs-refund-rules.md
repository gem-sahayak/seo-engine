# GeM Caution Money Deposit Slabs & Refund Rules (2026): ₹5k, ₹10k & ₹25k Tiers

Government e-Marketplace (GeM) portal par sellers ko active bid participation aur catalog listing ke liye mandatory **Caution Money Deposit** maintain karna padta hai. Ye deposit seller integrity ensure karne aur frivolous bidding rokne ke liye laya gaya tha.

Is complete guide mein hum cover karenge **Caution Money turnover slabs**, **PFMS e-Wallet payment methods**, **forfeiture conditions**, aur **refund claim steps**.

---

## 💰 Official GeM Caution Money Turnover Slabs 2026

GeM Policy Directives ke mutabiq seller ke annual turnover ke aadhar par caution money deposit calculate hota hai:

| Seller Annual Turnover Slab | Caution Money Deposit Required | Target Seller Category |
|---|---|---|
| **Less than ₹50 Lakhs (< ₹50L)** | **₹5,000** | Micro & Small Enterprises, New Startups |
| **₹50 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore (₹50L - ₹1Cr)** | **₹10,000** | Small & Medium Enterprises |
| **Above ₹1 Crore (> ₹1Cr)** | **₹25,000** | Large Enterprises, Mid-Corporates, OEMs |

> 📌 **Note**: Caution money ek baar hi deposit karni hoti hai. Jab tak aapka turnover slab change nahi hota ya deposit forfeit nahi hota, aapko dobara pay nahi karna padta.

---

## 💳 Step-by-Step Caution Money Deposit Process

1. GeM Portal (`gem.gov.in`) par Primary User credentials se Login karein.
2. Top Menu mein **My Account** -> **Caution Money** section par click karein.
3. Select Bank Node (ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, SBI portal nodes).
4. System aapke registered turnover basis par auto-calculated slab (₹5,000 / ₹10,000 / ₹25,000) display karega.
5. Net Banking / NEFT / RTGS se payment complete karein.
6. Payment succeed hote hi status **Deposit Active (100%)** reflect hone lagega.

## 🚨 Grounds for Caution Money Forfeiture (Kab Deposit Zabt Hota Hai)

GeM Terms of Use Clause 11 ke mutabiq caution money in conditions mein forfeit (zabt) kiya ja sakta hai:

1. **L1 Withdrawal**: Order L1 aane ke baad agar seller contract accept karne se manaa kar deta hai.
2. **Fake Documents**: Fake Udyam, PAN, GST, ya Brand Authorization upload karne par.
3. **Repeated Order Failure**: Delivered items 3 se zyada baar rejection hona.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: Kya Caution Money refundable hai?
Haan, agar aap GeM account permanently close/deactivate karte hain aur aapka koi pending contract ya active penalty nahi hai, toh Caution Money 100% original bank account mein refund ho jati hai.

### Q2: Turnover badhne par caution money top-up kaise karein?
Jab aap ITR file karke profile mein turnover upgrade karenge, toh system automatic remaining balance amount (e.g. ₹5,000 se ₹10,000 ke liye ₹5,000 extra) deposit karne ka prompt dikhayega.
