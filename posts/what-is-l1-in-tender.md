## 1. L1 Status Kya Hota Hai? (Definition & Core Concept)

Public Procurement Regulations (GFR 2017) ke mutabiq, **L1** ka full form hai **Lowest Bidder 1**.

Jab government department kisi product ya service ke liye tender nikalta hai, tab bid evaluation do stages me hota hai:
1. **Technical Bid Stage:** Pehle sabhi bidders ke documents, certificates, turnover, aur specifications verify kiye jate hain. Non-eligible bidders ko reject kar diya jata hai.
2. **Financial Bid Stage (Price Opening):** Technically qualified bidders ki financial rates open ki jaati hain. System sabhi offers ko ascending order me rank karta hai:
   - **L1 Rank:** Sabse kam price quote karne wala bidder.
   - **L2 Rank:** Doosri sabse kam price quote karne wala bidder.
   - **L3 Rank:** Teesri sabse kam price quote karne wala bidder.

Standard rule ke mutabiq, buyer **L1 bidder** ko contract award karne ke liye obligated hota hai.

## 2. GeM Portal Par L1 Evaluation Kaise Hota Hai?

GeM portal par financial opening fully automated hoti hai. Bids ki opening date ke baad system instantly **L1 Summary Matrix** generate karta hai.

### GeM L1 Evaluation Formula:
Commercial price evaluation me pure item rate ke sath-sath GST, Inland Freight, Delivery Charges, aur Warranty Services ko milakar **Total Landed Cost** calculate ki jaati hai:

$$\text{Total Quoted Price } (P_{Total}) = (\text{Unit Base Price} + \text{Freight Charges}) \times \text{Quantity} + \text{GST Appliable}$$

System jiske $P_{Total}$ ko minimum pata hai, use L1 declare kar deta hai.

## 3. MSE Purchase Preference & Split Order Rules (L1 + 15% Band)

Aksar sellers sochte hain ki agar unki rank L2 ya L3 aati hai, toh unhe order nahi milega. Lekin **Public Procurement Policy for MSMEs** ke under specific benefits milte hain:

```
Situation: Non-MSME Firm is L1 (Price = ₹100)
MSME Firm is L2 (Price = ₹112, within L1 + 15% Price Band)
Benefit: MSME seller to L1 price (₹100) match karne ka option diya jata hai.
Result: Price match karne par minimum 25% order quantity MSME seller ko award hoti hai.
```

### Key Highlights of MSE Preference:
- **Price Band:** Agar MSME seller ki quote L1 price ke **15% ke andar** hai (i.e. $P_{MSME} \le P_{L1} \times 1.15$).
- **Quantity Share:** MSME seller L1 price match karke total order volume ka **25% (jis me 4% SC/ST aur 3% Women MSME reserved)** claim kar sakta hai.

---

## 4. Counter-Offer Process (Jab L1 Bidder Order Reject Kare)

Kabhi-kabhi L1 bidder raw material price rise ya stock unavailability ki wajah se GeM order accept karne se refuse kar deta hai. Is case me buyer contract cancel karne ke bajaye **Counter-Offer** process initiate karta hai:

1. Buyer L2 bidder ko official email notification bhejta hai.
2. L2 bidder ko **L1 Price Match** karne ke liye system window milta hai.
3. Agar L2 bidder L1 price match karne ke liye agree kar leta hai, toh baaki order volume L2 bidder ko award kar diya jata hai.
